Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Ownerdeck is operated by Mark Saade in Cyprus. This explains what data is collected, why, and what rights you have under the GDPR. It is written to be read, not to hide behind length.

1. Who we are

Data controller: Mark Saade, trading as Ownerdeck.
Address: Livadia, Larnaca, Cyprus
Registration: sole trader — no company registration number
VAT: not VAT registered, so no VAT is charged
Jurisdiction: Cyprus
Contact: mark@ownerdeck.com

There is no Data Protection Officer. The business is below the threshold at which one is required, and Mark handles all requests personally.

2. What is collected

Enquiry details — your name, business name and WhatsApp number when you contact us about the service.

Conversation data — messages exchanged between your customers and your assistant, together with the bookings it captures. This is processed only to run the assistant on your behalf. It belongs to you.

Usage data — anonymous page views collected by Vercel Analytics. Aggregated, no personal identifiers, no cookies.

Website chat — if you use the chat assistant on this website, the text you type is sent to Anthropic to generate a reply. Type nothing into it you would not put in a first email. It is a sales demo, not a support channel, and it is not the same system that runs a customer's assistant.

IP address — your IP is processed when you use the website chat, to count requests against a rate limit and to run an anti-bot check. It is used for nothing else and is not stored alongside message content.

3. Why it is used, and on what legal basis

Each purpose below names its lawful basis under Article 6 of the GDPR.

  • To set up, train and operate your assistant — contract (Art. 6(1)(b))
  • To notify you of bookings and issues — contract (Art. 6(1)(b))
  • To answer enquiries and provide support — legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): responding to someone who contacted us
  • To improve the service using aggregated, anonymised data — legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f))
  • To answer questions in the website chat — legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): answering a prospective customer who chose to start the conversation
  • To rate-limit and screen the website chat for bots — legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): keeping the service available and preventing abuse of a paid API

Where a purpose rests on legitimate interests you may object at any time — see section 7.

4. Who it is shared with

Your data is never sold. It is shared only with the processors below, each for one purpose:

  • WhatsApp / Meta — message delivery, under Meta's own terms
  • Vercel (USA) — website hosting and anonymous analytics
  • Anthropic (USA) — generates the replies in the website chat. Anthropic does not use data submitted through its API to train its models.
  • Cloudflare (USA) — serves the site and runs the Turnstile anti-bot check, which processes your IP address
  • Upstash (EU, Frankfurt) — holds short-lived rate-limit counters derived from your IP
  • Legal authorities — only where required by law

Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to train third-party models.

5. Transfers outside the EEA

Anthropic, Vercel and Cloudflare are established in the United States, so using this website involves transferring some data there. Those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, incorporated into each provider's data processing terms.

Rate-limit data stays in the EEA (Frankfurt). A customer's own conversation data is held in the systems used to run their assistant and is not sent to the United States for any purpose other than generating replies.

6. Automated replies and AI

The chat on this website is answered by an AI assistant, not a person. It is labelled as such in the chat window. It cannot make decisions about you, and nothing it says forms a contract — prices and terms are what this website and your written agreement say they are.

Where Ownerdeck runs an assistant on a customer's WhatsApp number, that customer is responsible for telling their own customers they may be talking to an automated assistant.

7. How long it is kept

Data is retained while your assistant is active. If you cancel, personal data is deleted within 30 days and conversation logs within 90 days, unless the law requires otherwise.

Website chat messages are not stored by Ownerdeck beyond the length of the conversation in your own browser tab. Rate-limit counters expire automatically after one hour. Anthropic retains API inputs for a limited period under its own terms for abuse monitoring.

8. Your rights

As an EU resident you may access, correct, erase, export, restrict, or object to the processing of your data, and you may withdraw consent where processing is based on it. You may also complain to the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection, or to the supervisory authority where you live.

To exercise any of these, email mark@ownerdeck.com. You will get a reply within 30 days, usually much sooner.

9. Cookies

Ownerdeck sets no tracking cookies. Analytics are cookieless, so there is no consent banner. A single browser preference is stored locally to remember your light or dark theme choice — it never leaves your device.

If you open the website chat, Cloudflare Turnstile may set a short-lived token to confirm you are not a bot. It is strictly necessary to protect the service and is not used for tracking or advertising.

10. Security

All traffic is served over HTTPS. Conversation logs are restricted to you and to Mark. Access is reviewed regularly.

11. Changes

Material changes will be communicated by email. The date at the top reflects the latest revision.

Questions about any of this? Email mark@ownerdeck.com — you will reach Mark directly, not a support queue.
