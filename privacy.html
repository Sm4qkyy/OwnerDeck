Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 May 2026

OwnerDeck ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Mark Saade, based in Cyprus. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who we are

Data controller: Mark Saade, trading as OwnerDeck
Contact: mark@ownerdeck.com
Address: Larnaca, Cyprus

2. Data we collect

Contact & enquiry data — name, business name, WhatsApp number, and any information you provide when submitting a contact form or enquiring about the service.

Conversation data — WhatsApp message content exchanged between your customers and your AI assistant, processed solely to operate and improve the assistant on your behalf. This data belongs to you; we process it only to deliver the service.

Usage data — anonymous page-view and performance data collected by Vercel Analytics. This data is aggregated, contains no personal identifiers, and does not use cookies.

3. How we use your data

  • To set up, train, and operate your WhatsApp booking assistant (legal basis: contract performance)
  • To send you booking notifications and operational updates (legal basis: contract performance)
  • To respond to enquiries and support requests (legal basis: legitimate interests)
  • To improve the service using aggregated, anonymised analytics (legal basis: legitimate interests)

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with:

  • Vercel — hosting and anonymous analytics (EU-safe, no personal data)
  • WhatsApp / Meta — message delivery via WhatsApp Business API, subject to Meta's terms
  • Legal authorities — if required by law

5. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. On cancellation, we delete your personal data within 30 days and your property data within 90 days, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

As an EU resident you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data
  • Request portability of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection

To exercise any of these rights, email mark@ownerdeck.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

OwnerDeck does not use tracking cookies. Vercel Analytics is cookieless. No consent banner is required.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to property and owner data is restricted to authorised OwnerDeck personnel only. We review our security practices regularly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by email. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email mark@ownerdeck.com.
