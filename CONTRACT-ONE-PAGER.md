# Ownerdeck — one-page agreement

**Draft. Not legal advice, and not reviewed by a lawyer.** Every term below
is already published on ownerdeck.com; this assembles them into one page a
client can sign, so nothing here is a new promise. Before using it on a real
client, have a Cyprus lawyer read it — particularly the liability and
data-processing clauses, which are the two a template gets wrong.

---

**Between:** Mark Saade, sole trader, Livadia, Larnaca, Cyprus ("Ownerdeck")
**And:** ............................................................ ("you")
**Date:** ....................

---

### 1. What we are building
Plan: ☐ Site ☐ Deck ☐ Full Deck ☐ Custom (attach the quote)

The plan you have chosen is described on ownerdeck.com/pricing on the date of
signing. A copy of that page as it stood is attached and forms part of this
agreement, so neither of us is relying on a page that may change later.

### 2. What it costs
- **To build:** €.......... one-off.
- **To run:** €.......... per month, starting the month after it goes live.
- **No VAT.** Ownerdeck is not registered for VAT, so the price shown is the
  price you pay.
- **No commission.** We take no percentage of your bookings. Deposits and
  payments run through your own provider and your own account; we never hold
  your customers' money.

### 3. Paying for the build
☐ In full before work starts
☐ In stages: ..........% to start, ..........% when you approve the design,
  ..........% when it goes live

An optional €75 deposit holds a build slot. It is refundable in full until
work starts and is credited against the build fee.

### 4. Stopping
- You can stop on any plan with **one month's notice**, in writing, by email.
- **There is no minimum term.**
- When you stop, you keep the site files and an export of your database. We
  hand both over free of charge, within 14 days of the notice period ending.
- Your domain, your Google listing, your WhatsApp number and your payment
  provider account are yours throughout and are never held as leverage.

### 5. What the monthly covers
Hosting, the domain and certificate, the database, the assistant, backups,
security updates, and the changes you ask for. Changes are requested by
message and are not billed separately.

### 6. Your data, and your customers' data
- You remain the **controller** of your customers' personal data. Ownerdeck
  acts as your **processor** and only on your instructions.
- We do not sell your data or your customers' data, and we do not use it to
  train anything.
- Conversations stay in your own WhatsApp and in a private log you can read.
- Either of us may end this agreement immediately if the other is required to
  by a data-protection authority.
- A fuller description is at ownerdeck.com/privacy.

### 7. What we do not promise
We do not promise a number of bookings, a search ranking, or a revenue
figure. Anything either of us has said about results elsewhere is an
observation, not a term of this agreement.

### 8. If something goes wrong
Tell us. We fix it. If we cannot, clause 4 applies and you leave with your
files and your data. Our total liability is limited to the fees you have paid
in the previous three months. *(Flag for the lawyer: enforceability of this
cap under Cyprus consumer law.)*

### 9. Law
This agreement is governed by the law of the Republic of Cyprus.

## Notes for Mark, not for the client

- **Section 3 is the only unresolved decision.** The site does not currently
  offer milestone payments anywhere. Pick a split before this goes out, and
  put the same split on the pricing page — offering it in the contract but
  not on the site means it only reaches people who already asked, which is
  the opposite of the point.
- Everything else here is already public on the site, so signing it commits
  you to nothing you were not already promising.
- The two clauses that need a lawyer rather than a proofread are **6** (the
  processor relationship, since you handle other businesses' customer
  conversations) and **8** (the liability cap).
