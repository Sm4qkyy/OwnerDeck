---

Build me a complete brand asset sheet for **Ownerdeck** as a design component.
Match this brand exactly — it is already live, so do not reinterpret it.

**What Ownerdeck is**
An AI assistant that answers a business's customers on their own WhatsApp
number. It replies in about two seconds, in the customer's language, quotes
real prices and live availability from the owner's own system, then captures
the booking and notifies the owner. It runs today on a Limassol car rental
company's WhatsApp. The buyer is an owner-operator in Cyprus: practical,
sceptical of software, reads on a phone.

**Voice:** calm, precise, technical-but-plain, quietly confident. Never hypey,
never cute.

**Colour — light theme**
- Accent teal `#01c0b8` (buttons, the chat bubble in the logo)
- Teal as text `#097f7a` (darker, so it passes contrast on white)
- Accent wash `#e6faf8`, accent line `#a4f4ec`
- Page `#ffffff`, card `#ffffff`, banded section `#f6f7f9`
- Ink `#0f1115`, body `#3d4448`, muted `#6b7478`, faint `#8b9497`
- Border `#e5e8ea`

**Colour — dark theme**
- Accent teal `#01c0b8` (unchanged), teal as text `#38dccd` (brighter)
- Page `#0a0e0f`, card `#121819`, banded section `#0e1314`
- Ink `#eef4f4`, body `#c8d4d4`, muted `#9fb0b2`, faint `#6b7b7d`
- Border `#243032`

**Type**
- Headings: Space Grotesk, weight 600, letter-spacing −0.03em
- Body: IBM Plex Sans
- Labels/eyebrows: IBM Plex Mono, uppercase, letterspaced, prefixed with a
  teal `//`

**Shape:** 16px card radius, 10–12px controls, 999px pills, 1px hairline
borders, soft shadows only. A faint 24px dotted grid is the signature texture.

**The logo**
A deck of stacked cards — three rounded rectangles receding in slight
perspective, front card solid with a crisp outline, the two behind
progressively fainter — with a rounded chat bubble rising from the top card,
filled solid teal, with a small tail. Wordmark is lowercase `ownerdeck`, one
word, with `deck` in teal.

**Produce all of the following, laid out on one sheet, each labelled:**
1. Primary horizontal lockup (mark + wordmark) — on light and on dark
2. Mark only, square
3. Stacked lockup (mark above wordmark)
4. All-white knockout and all-black single-colour versions
5. Favicon grid at 512 / 256 / 64 / 32 / 16 px, showing what simplifies away
6. Social profile picture, 500×500, circle-safe
7. LinkedIn banner, 1584×396 — **the left third must stay empty**, because
   the circular profile picture overlaps there; all content sits right of
   x≈580 and ends before x≈1450
8. Open Graph share card, 1200×630
9. Instagram post set, four 1080×1080 squares as one system, each carrying a
   single statement:
   "It's 11pm. They're asking about tomorrow." /
   "Answered in 2 seconds. In their language." /
   "Your real prices. Your live availability." /
   "You wake up to the booking."
10. Instagram story template, 1080×1920, content inside the middle 80%
11. WhatsApp Business avatar, 640×640, teal ground with a white mark
12. An 8-icon line family on a 24px grid, 1.6px stroke, rounded caps: chat
    bubble with bolt; clock with moon; globe with speech marks; price tag;
    calendar with check; car key; phone with notification dot; handover arrow
13. Business card, 85×55mm, front and back — contact details:
    +357 96 922259 · mark@ownerdeck.com · ownerdeck.com

**Rules — do not break these**
- Teal is the *only* accent. One accent against monochrome. Never add a
  second brand hue.
- Teal used as text must darken on light (`#097f7a`) and brighten on dark
  (`#38dccd`). Pure `#01c0b8` text on white fails contrast.
- The wordmark is lowercase `ownerdeck`, one word, `deck` in teal.
  Not "OwnerDeck", not "Owner Deck".
- Never recolour third-party logos — WhatsApp stays green, Instagram keeps
  its gradient.
- Everything must survive at 32px. If a detail turns to mush, simplify the
  master rather than shipping it.
- No cartoon robots, no "AI brain" imagery, no purple SaaS gradients, no
  stock photography, no 3D renders, no glassmorphism.
