FAQ

Questions you'll probably ask.

Do you need my Airbnb password?
No. We connect via Airbnb's official API and your PMS (Hostaway, Guesty, Hospitable, etc.) using read-only credentials. We never touch your listings, your pricing, or your guest conversations unless you explicitly enable guest comms automation.
What if I don't use a PMS?
That's fine — most of our early clients don't. We connect directly to your Airbnb and Booking.com accounts. If you eventually move to a PMS, we already integrate with the major ones.
How long does setup take?
About a week from kickoff call to first live report. The longest part is approving your first sample report — getting the branding, sections, and tone right for your business.
Can my owners log in and see the dashboard themselves?
On the Growth plan, yes — each owner gets their own white-labeled portal where they can view past reports and current month performance. On the Starter plan, owners receive the monthly PDF only.
What about languages? My owners speak Greek/Russian/German.
Reports can be generated in English, Greek, Russian, German, French, and Italian. You can also have different languages per owner — useful for managers with international owner portfolios.
What's the free first month catch?
No catch. We set you up for free in exchange for honest feedback during onboarding and a short testimonial if you're happy after 30 days. If it doesn't work for your business, you walk. Available for the first 10 Cyprus clients.
