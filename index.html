The problem

Reporting is the part of the job no one signed up for.

You started managing properties because you're good with owners and good with guests. Not because you wanted to spend half of every month exporting CSVs and copy-pasting numbers into Excel.

  • The 1st-of-the-month panic

    Screenshotting Airbnb dashboards, exporting Booking CSVs, formatting another spreadsheet that looks slightly different from last month's.

  • Owners asking the same questions

    "How did we do last month?" "Why was the payout lower?" "When are my next bookings?" — answered manually, twelve times over.

  • Guests messaging at 11pm

    WiFi password, check-in code, parking, restaurant recommendations. Repeated every single booking. Forever.

  • Reviews that never get asked for

    You meant to message them after checkout. You didn't. Your rating stagnates while competitors automate the ask.

About

Built in Cyprus, for the people running the buildings.

OwnerDeck started because every property manager I spoke with described the same week-long ritual at the end of each month: copy-paste, screenshot, format, send, repeat.

The big PMS platforms either don't solve it or do it badly — generic templates that look nothing like the manager's brand, with the wrong data fields, in the wrong order.

So we built something narrow on purpose. Two jobs, done seriously: owner reports that look like they came from a Fortune 500 hotel chain, and guest communication that runs in the background.

We're based in Cyprus, we work with managers across the island and Greece, and we keep the scope small so the product stays sharp.
