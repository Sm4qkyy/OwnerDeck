How it works

One conversation, start to confirmed booking — no human in the loop.

1

Customer messages your WhatsApp

Same number they already use. No new app, no form, no waiting till morning.

2

AI detects language & replies

English, Russian, German, Greek and more — auto-detected and mirrored, in ~2 seconds.

3

Checks your live availability

Calls your real booking system. Only shows what's actually available on those dates, at that day's real price.

4

Builds the quote

Duration × rate, plus add-ons and extras. Accurate, not guessed from memory.

5

Confirms & notifies you

Collects only what's missing, sends a booking reference, and pings you the moment it lands.
