Questions

The things owners ask first.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. It runs on the WhatsApp number you already give out. Your customers message the same number they always have, and nothing on your cards or listings has to change.

What if it says something wrong?

It quotes only from your own prices and your own availability, never from memory. When a question falls outside what you have told it, it says so and passes the conversation to you rather than guessing. You see every one of those in the log.

Can I take over a conversation?

Any time. You reply in the chat and it steps back. Hand it back whenever you are done. It is your WhatsApp — you are never locked out of your own conversation.

What languages does it speak?

It detects the customer's language and replies in it. If someone starts in English and switches to Russian halfway, it switches too, in the same thread.

Does it work with my booking system?

It reads live availability and prices from the system you already use, so it cannot sell something that is gone. If your availability lives in a spreadsheet, that works too. We check yours before you commit to anything.

How long does setup take?

Days, not weeks. You tell me what you offer, your prices, locations and policies once — that is your part. I build it, test it and switch it on. A brand new business, website and all, takes about a week.

What happens when my prices change?

Change them where you already keep them. It quotes from the live source every time, so a July rate never turns up in August. For policy changes — a new delivery fee, a different deposit — message me and it is done.

Is my customer data safe?

Conversations stay in your WhatsApp and in a log only you and I can open. Nothing is sold, nothing is shared with other operators, and nothing is used to train anything. If you leave, the log is yours.

What if I want to stop?

Message me and it is off. No notice period and no exit fee on Answer. Your number, your customers and your conversation history stay exactly where they are.

Who is behind this?

Mark. I build each one and set it up personally — I am not reselling someone else's product. You will have my WhatsApp, and you will be talking to the person who built the thing.
