Where we stop

And the things we do not do, plainly.

  • We are not a marketing agency. No ad campaigns, no social media management, no content calendar.
  • We do not handle your money. Deposits and payments go through your own account, never ours.
  • We do not build apps. A good website on a phone beats an app nobody downloads.
  • The assistant does not invent a price. It quotes from your database or it says it needs to check.
  • The assistant does not guess. Anything outside what it has been told goes to you, and you see every one of those.
  • We do not replace anyone on your team. The person answering at 11pm is you, and this gives you that hour back.

Questions

The things owners ask first.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

Not necessarily. If it looks fine and loads quickly, we can put the database and the assistant behind what you already have. We rebuild when the existing site is slow, unmaintainable, or impossible to keep current — and we will tell you honestly which of those yours is.

Who owns the website and the data?

You do. The domain stays in your name, the database contents are yours, and if you leave we hand over an export of everything. Nothing is held back to keep you paying.

Do I need a new phone number for the assistant?

No. It runs on the WhatsApp number you already give out, so your customers message the same number as always and nothing on your cards or listings has to change.

What if the assistant says something wrong?

It quotes only from your database, never from memory, so it cannot invent a price or sell something you do not have. When a question falls outside what it has been told, it says so and hands the conversation to you. You see every one of those in the log.

Can I take over a conversation myself?

Any time. You reply in the chat and it steps back, and you hand it back whenever you are done. It is your WhatsApp — you are never locked out of your own conversation.

What languages does it handle?

It detects the customer's language and replies in it. If someone opens in English and switches to Russian halfway through, it switches too, in the same thread. Nothing to configure.

How long does it take?

The assistant on its own is days. A website and database together is about a week from the first conversation, assuming you can get us your prices and photos. We do not disappear for a month and come back with a surprise.

What does the monthly actually cover?

Hosting, the database, the assistant running, backups, security updates, and the changes you ask for — a new service, a price change, a page that needs rewording. It is not a support contract you never use; it is us keeping the thing current.

What happens when my prices change?

Change them in one place. The website, the assistant and the booking confirmations all read from the same database, so a July rate never turns up in August on a page you forgot about.

Who is behind this?

Mark. I build each one and set it up personally — I am not reselling someone else's product. You will have my WhatsApp, and you will be talking to the person who built the thing.
