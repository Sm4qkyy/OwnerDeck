// Questions owners actually ask

The objections, answered straight.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. It runs on the WhatsApp number you already give out. Your customers message the same number they always have, and nothing on your business cards or listings has to change.

What if it says something wrong?

It quotes only from your own prices and your own availability — it doesn't answer from memory. When a question falls outside what you've told it, it says so and passes the conversation to you instead of guessing. You'll see those in the log.

Can I take over a conversation?

Yes, any time. You reply in the chat and it steps back. Hand it back whenever you're done. It's your WhatsApp — you're never locked out of your own conversation.

What languages does it speak?

It detects the customer's language and replies in it — English, Russian, Greek, German, Polish, Arabic, Hebrew and more. If a customer starts in English and switches to Russian halfway, it switches too, in the same thread.

Does it work with my booking system?

It reads live availability and prices from the system you already use, so it can't sell a car that's out. If your availability lives in a spreadsheet, that works too. We check yours on the first call, before you commit to anything.

How long does setup take?

Live in 48 hours. You tell me your fleet, rates, locations and policies once — that's your part. I build and test it, then switch it on.

What happens when my prices change?

Change them where you already keep them. It quotes from the live source every time, so a July rate never turns up in August. Policy changes — a new delivery fee, a different deposit — you message me and it's done.

Is my customer data safe?

Conversations stay in your WhatsApp and in a log only you and I can open. Nothing is sold, nothing is shared with other operators, and nothing is used to train anything. If you leave, the log is yours.

What if I want to stop?

Message me and it's off. No notice period, no contract, no exit fee. Your number, your customers and your conversation history stay exactly where they are.

Does it work for boats, buggies and transfers, not just cars?

Yes. Car rental is where it's running today, so that's the one I can show you. Anything with availability, a price and a booking works the same way — buggy and quad rental, boat charters, airport transfers, villa changeovers.

Who is behind this?

Mark. I build each assistant and set it up personally — I'm not reselling someone else's platform. You'll have my WhatsApp, and you'll be talking to the person who built the thing.
